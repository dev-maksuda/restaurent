About Our Story

A Kitchen Built on Passion & Freshness

Flavora started back in 2013 as a tiny kitchen with only six tables. Today we serve hundreds of happy guests every single day — but the promise is exactly the same: honest food, cooked fresh, served with a smile.

  • 100% fresh, hand-picked local ingredients
